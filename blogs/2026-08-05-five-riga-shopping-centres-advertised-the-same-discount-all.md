Shopping-centre radio in Latvia grew 54% in 30 days, from 2,506 spots to 3,861, with Sky&More and Teika Plaza arriving from zero. Five of six centres named the same figure, up to 70% off, and delivered it to completely different generations. Then on 3 August Akropole became the first advertiser in the category to move to the school year, with a donation drive carrying no discount at all.
